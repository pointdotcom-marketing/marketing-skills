# Point Illustration System

Use this reference when selecting, commissioning, generating, editing, or reviewing Point illustrations. It extends the general artwork direction in [visual-guidelines.md](visual-guidelines.md) with production guidance derived from approved Point examples and the supplied seasonal paid-social brief.

## Core visual language

- Build scenes from flat, simplified geometric forms.
- Use clean shapes with minimal or no outlines.
- Layer subtle grain or stippled texture over shapes without making the work muddy.
- Use soft gradients and restrained overlapping translucent shapes.
- Favor simplified homes, trees, people, roads, landscapes, and everyday objects.
- Use abstract circles, arches, curved landscapes, and sun or glow shapes as structural elements.
- Keep compositions crisp, uncluttered, modern, friendly, trustworthy, and premium.
- Preserve transparent backgrounds when illustrations are delivered as standalone assets.

## Palette

Use exact approved values from [visual-guidelines.md](visual-guidelines.md). Build primarily with muted blues, blue-grays, cream, white, and charcoal. Use `yorange` and related gold or orange tokens as restrained focal accents. Do not invent bright seasonal colors outside the approved palette.

## Texture and depth

- Keep grain subtle enough that shapes remain crisp at social-feed size.
- Use gradients and transparency to create depth without realistic lighting or 3D volume.
- Keep texture separate from critical silhouettes and small details when practical.
- Inspect exported raster artwork for banding, muddy overlays, and unintended opaque backgrounds.

## Subject treatment

- Keep homes and objects recognizable through silhouette and a few defining shapes.
- Simplify architecture; avoid unnecessary windows, trim, signage, or decorative detail.
- Keep people minimal and dignified. Do not use exaggerated cartoon anatomy or detailed character rendering.
- Use curved roads, landscapes, and overlapping fields to create movement and depth.
- Keep seasonal cues restrained so the work still belongs to the evergreen Point library.

## Reuse and originality

Inspect approved assets before creating new artwork. Reuse an existing approved illustration without redrawing it when it meets the assignment.

Treat images in `references/` as inspection material unless the visual-reference index explicitly marks them approved for reuse. Treat new illustrations as campaign drafts until a brand owner approves them for the reusable asset library.

## Prohibited treatments

Do not use:

- photography or photorealistic rendering
- watercolor or painterly effects
- 3D imagery
- generic AI-illustration aesthetics
- children's-book or cartoon styling
- thick black outlines
- bright off-palette colors
- black backgrounds created from transparency previews
- detailed character illustration or architecture
- text embedded in the illustration

## Review checklist

- The scene uses simple Point-like geometry rather than generic vector stock styling.
- Texture, gradients, and transparency are restrained and intentional.
- The palette uses exact approved Point values.
- The subject remains recognizable without excess detail.
- The composition preserves negative space for the surrounding layout.
- No text or legal copy is baked into the artwork.
- Standalone exports preserve transparency.
- Reused and newly created elements have documented source and approval status.
