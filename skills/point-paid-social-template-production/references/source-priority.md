# Source Priority

Use this order to resolve instructions and source material. Preserve the distinction between visual style, reusable assets, current copy, and compliance approval.

## Visual decisions

1. Current campaign instructions explicitly approved as an exception
2. Current Point visual-brand rules and approved production assets
3. Supplied visual references with documented reuse status
4. Historical creative used only to understand composition or treatment
5. General design judgment

Do not interpret a campaign brief's silence as permission to override the Point brand system. When a campaign instruction conflicts with a brand rule, apply an explicit exception already authorized by the user or brand owner and record it. If precedence is unresolved, identify the conflict, continue unaffected work, and request only the missing decision before final delivery.

## Copy, claims, and legal language

1. Current Legal-approved language or current approved claim source
2. Current campaign brief, limited to language explicitly supplied for this assignment
3. Placeholders that clearly identify the missing approval

Never treat historical ads, screenshots, mockups, ratings, testimonials, footer copy, or reference layouts as current factual or legal sources.

## Asset status

Classify each supplied visual file as one of:

- **Approved for reuse:** May be placed in the deliverable without redrawing.
- **Reference only:** May guide style, hierarchy, texture, or composition but may not be copied into the deliverable.
- **Unverified:** Do not use in final production until ownership and permission are confirmed.
- **Generated draft:** Keep in the assignment output until reviewed; do not promote it into a reusable skill asset automatically.

Record the source, owner when known, approval status, and intended use in the visual-reference index or delivery notes.
