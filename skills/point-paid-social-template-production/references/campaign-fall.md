This reference captures the supplied fall-template assignment. Treat it as a campaign brief, not as a permanent change to Point's global visual rules.

## Shared deliverables

Create:

1. one editable PowerPoint containing both templates as separate slides
2. one native Canva-editable version when Canva creation and import can be verified; otherwise a PowerPoint prepared for clean Canva import
3. individual `1080 × 1350` PNG previews
4. separate transparent PNG files for each newly created illustration
5. a short style guide listing fonts, substitutions, colors, and spacing

Use `1080 × 1350` or a matching `4:5` PowerPoint slide ratio.

Keep the Point logo, headline, subheader, benefit copy, checkmarks or icons, legal footer, and main illustration separate and editable. Do not bake copy into an illustration.

## Shared benefits

Use these three editable benefits in a consistent row beneath the main illustration:

- Cash for what matters most.
- No monthly payments.
- No impact to your mortgage.

Use an approved Point gold checkmark when available. Otherwise, use a simple restrained checkmark that fits the supplied illustration system and identify it as newly created.

## Template 1: Fall Apple Trees

### Copy

Headline:

> Your home can bring more to the table.

Subheader:

> More freedom. More possibilities.
>
> More of what matters most.

### Illustration direction

Create an original fall apple-tree scene in the supplied Point illustration system. Include:

- one or two simplified apple trees
- abstract geometric leaves in Point blue, muted gold, and soft orange
- small circular apples
- a Point-style home silhouette in the background
- a simple geometric basket of apples
- soft overlapping landscape shapes
- an abstract gold sun or circular glow
- subtle grain and stippled texture

Keep the scene warm, seasonal, and homeowner-focused. Do not make it resemble a realistic orchard, watercolor painting, children's-book illustration, or food advertisement.

## Template 2: Fall Neighborhood and School Bus

### Copy

Headline:

> Imagine what’s possible.

Subheader:

> Your home equity could help you get there.

### Illustration direction

Create an original fall neighborhood scene in the supplied Point illustration system. Include:

- a simplified geometric yellow school bus
- a curved blue road that creates forward movement
- Point-style home silhouettes
- simplified fall trees
- abstract gold, blue, and cream landscape shapes
- a soft gold sun or circular glow
- a few minimal falling leaves
- subtle grain and stippled texture

Keep the bus and homes recognizable while preserving simple geometry. Do not include realistic people, school signs, detailed architecture, a school building, or explicit back-to-school language.

## Typography requested by the campaign brief

- Primary headline: ITC Century Std Book.
- Approved Century alternative: Century.
- Body copy and benefits: Circular Std.
- Italic subheader: Lora Italic.
- ITC headline line spacing: `1.05`.
- Century headline line spacing: `1.20`.
- Body and subheader line spacing: `1.40`; increase body copy to `1.50` only when crowded.

Keep all text editable. When an exact font is unavailable, use the closest available fallback and identify the substitution in the style guide. Treat the line-spacing values as campaign-specific pending resolution against the current global brand rule.

## Layout direction

- Use the supplied Point ads as layout references only.
- Place the Point logo at the top.
- Keep the headline large and centered.
- Give the subheader comfortable spacing below the headline.
- Make the illustration the main visual focus.
- Place the benefits in a consistent row near the bottom.
- Match the legal-footer treatment while using current approved legal copy.
- Maintain generous, consistent margins and paid-social safe areas.
- Prevent single-word headline widows.
- Avoid clipped copy, unintended overlaps, inconsistent alignment, distortion, and awkward crops.
- Keep the composition simple and avoid unnecessary decoration.

## Illustration guardrails

Match the supplied Point illustration library:

- flat simplified geometric forms
- clean shapes with minimal or no outlines
- subtle grain and stippled texture
- soft gradients and overlapping translucent shapes
- limited Point brand palette
- simplified homes, trees, people, and objects
- abstract circles, arches, curved landscapes, and sun shapes
- modern, friendly, premium compositions
- transparent backgrounds for standalone illustration assets

Use dark navy, slate and medium Point blues, soft periwinkle, restrained warm yellow or gold, cream, white, and small amounts of muted orange for fall. Use exact approved color values from `point-brand-guidelines`; do not invent replacements from these descriptive names.

Black areas visible around transparency-preview PNGs are not backgrounds. Do not include them in final artwork.

Do not use photography, photorealism, watercolor, 3D rendering, detailed characters, generic AI illustration styling, cartoon styling, thick black outlines, bright off-palette colors, black backgrounds, embedded text, school buildings, or explicit back-to-school language.
