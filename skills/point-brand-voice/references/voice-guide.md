# Point Brand Voice Guide

## Audience Context

Point's audience includes homeowners who may:

- Have meaningful home equity but limited monthly cash flow.
- Want to pay down high-interest debt.
- Need home repairs or improvements.
- Be retired, self-employed, rebuilding credit, or navigating an income constraint.
- Be comparing Point with a traditional loan, refinance, reverse mortgage, or personal loan.
- Feel cautious because the product is tied to their home.

Write with respect for the importance of the decision.

## Tone By Situation

Landing page hero:

- Crisp, benefit-led, and easy to understand in seconds.
- Pair aspiration with a specific product promise.

Product education:

- Calm, explanatory, and transparent.
- Use sequence: what it is, how it works, why it may fit, what to consider.

Comparison copy:

- Neutral and helpful.
- Show fit by need, not winner/loser language.

CTA copy:

- Low-pressure and action-oriented.
- Use "see", "check", "view", "compare", "learn", and "estimate".

Examples:

- "View my offers"
- "Check my eligibility"
- "Compare my options"
- "Get my cost estimate"
- "See what I qualify for"

Error or rejection copy:

- Helpful, not final-sounding unless final.
- Explain what happened and what the homeowner can do next.

## Word Bank

Prefer:

- access
- unlock
- home equity
- home wealth
- options
- fit
- flexible
- transparent
- upfront funds
- lump sum
- line of credit
- no monthly payments
- low monthly payments
- no prepayment penalty
- compare
- qualify
- eligibility
- step-by-step
- home equity experts

Use carefully:

- freedom: works best in human benefit copy; do not overuse.
- partnership: accurate for HEI; avoid for HELOC.
- investment: accurate for HEI; do not call HELOC an investment.
- no risk: only for prequalification/no credit impact contexts.
- fast: pair with conditions, such as "for qualified homeowners" or "as little as".

Avoid:

- desperate
- trapped
- bailout
- rescue
- guaranteed
- instant approval
- free cash
- cash out now
- best option for everyone
- no strings attached
- risk-free financing
- debt-free guaranteed

For product claims and acceptance criteria, follow the parent skill. Use [examples.md](../examples.md) for before/after calibration.
